The challenge
We are Team Vibecoders. This is TidePlan, a working microloan cash-flow planning prototype. A missed date is not always a missing ability to repay. We help lenders and borrowers agree on when repayment is genuinely affordable. This demo uses synthetic data and moves no real money.

See the cash-flow pattern
We start with Asha, a smallholder farmer. Her history shows seasonal income. A fixed six-month repayment breaches her protected cash buffer twice. TidePlan aligns the same eighteen-thousand-rupee obligation to conservative monthly capacity, keeping every month above her selected buffer.

Test affordability openly
In Plan Studio, the buffer protects essentials and existing debt before repayment is allocated. Change the buffer or apply an income shock, and compare fixed and aligned schedules immediately. The model uses transparent TypeScript rules and a conservative income haircut, not an unexplained credit score.

A safe failure matters
At a sixty-percent shock, there is not enough safe capacity to settle the loan. TidePlan shows the unscheduled balance and disables approval. That is intentional: a flexible schedule cannot create income. It signals human review, support, or separately assessed restructuring.

Agree to an exact plan
We return to a manageable scenario. The lender approves the exact payments, then the borrower sees the same monthly schedule and explicitly accepts it. Change the data, safety buffer, or shock, and the approval no longer matches. What-if analysis never silently changes an agreed plan.

Keep evidence, not theatre
The Trust Ledger records approval and consent as a linked SHA-256 history. We verify the chain, test a tampered copy, and export decision evidence. A simulated reserve can support an approved shock scenario, but it transfers no funds. Blockchain contracts are future integration blueprints, not live infrastructure.

How it is built
React and TypeScript power the interface and cash-flow engine. Recharts makes the alternatives visible, Papa Parse validates local CSV data, and GitHub Pages hosts the product. TidePlan is built for a responsible pilot: measure affordability, recovery, forecast error, and borrower cost together. Team Vibecoders: repayment in rhythm with life.
